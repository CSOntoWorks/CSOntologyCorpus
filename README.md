This repository contains the supporting dataset for the study **“Cybersecurity Ontologies: A Survey of Coverage, Availability, and Gaps against the EC JRC Taxonomy.”**
The dataset consolidates cybersecurity ontologies identified through nine systematic literature reviews. A total of 265 papers were examined, resulting in 231 unique ontology records. Each ontology was assigned a stable corpus identifier, linked to its source publication, and mapped to its primary domain in the EC JRC Cybersecurity Taxonomy. Public semantic availability was also checked; 26 ontologies had a publicly accessible machine-readable representation at the time of assessment.

## Limitations

Semantic-resource links reflect availability at the time of assessment and may later change or become unavailable. Domain assignments represent the ontology’s primary focus in the corpus; an ontology may also contain concepts relevant to other EC JRC domains.
